Baby and toddler sleep help

Start with what your family is seeing.

Sleep challenges rarely feel tidy. These guides help you name the pattern, understand what Emily would explore first, and choose a sensible next step without assuming every child needs the same approach.

My baby wakes shortly after bedtime

A wake 30–45 minutes after falling asleep is often called a false start. Explore schedule, environment, and sleep-association questions that may be involved.

Read about baby false starts →

My child needs the same help every time

Rocking, feeding, movement, sound, or another familiar cue may become part of how a child expects to fall asleep or resettle.

Understand sleep associations →

I need help soothing my newborn

My toddler or older child is struggling

Bedtime resistance, changing routines, and developing independence can keep sleep challenging beyond infancy.
